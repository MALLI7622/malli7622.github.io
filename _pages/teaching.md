## Teaching Assistant – Advanced Qualitative Research Analysis (Executive Ph.D.)
**Indian School of Business (ISB)**  
*Spring 2022*

I served as a Teaching Assistant for the Executive Ph.D. course Advanced Qualitative Research Analysis taught by `Professor Sumeet Kumar`, where I supported first year doctoral student in integrating machine learning and computational methods into qualitative and mixed-methods research.

My responsibilities included:
- Taught foundational machine learning concepts like clustering, topic modeling, representation learning tailored for qualitative research settings.
- Designing and delivering hands-on coding sessions in Python, focusing on text analysis, data preprocessing, and exploratory NLP techniques.
- Helping students bridge qualitative methodologies with ML-driven analysis, enabling scalable pattern discovery from interviews, documents, and textual datasets.
- Providing individual technical mentoring to support reproducible research workflows and computational rigor.
- Collaborating with faculty to align ML components with course objectives and research assignments.
